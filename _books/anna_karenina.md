The first line of Anna Karenina, “All happy families are alike, but every unhappy family is unhappy in its own way.” illuminates itself as a guiding principle for the exciting field of complex systems biology. Much like families, healthy people are healthy in the same way, yet those who become ill are often riddled with vastly different pathologies, especially in the context of aging and neurodegeneration.
