I am a Sophomore at Brown University looking to concentrate in Computational Neuroscience. I've been working in the [Wilson Lab](https://wilsonresearchlab.org/) at the Carney Institute for Brain Science developing models to harness the diagnostic and prognostic abilities of blood biomarkers in the context of neurodegeneration. 

Broadly, I am interested in blood biomarkers and complex systems biology. I aspire to use these methods in tandem to reveal how molecular and systems-level insights into neurodegeneration, acquired brain injuries, aging, and disease can help build models that can inform more effective therapies, both for retroaction and prevention.
